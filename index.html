Equivariance under u⊗x

A layer that multiplies the input by a weight on the right, x⊗W, commutes with multiplying every input on the left by a unit quaternion u. A norm-based activation keeps that property. A componentwise ReLU breaks it. The numbers are ‖f(u⊗x) − u⊗f(x)‖ on random inputs.

Monge-Ampère on ℂ²

For a Kähler potential that depends only on t = r² = |z₁|² + |z₂|², the complex Monge-Ampère determinant is K′(K′ + tK″). A Ricci-flat metric needs it to be constant. The legacy engine instead took a 1×1 Hessian in r, which is just φ″(r), and never compared it to anything.

Eguchi-Hanson is computed from its closed-form K′(t). The Taub-NUT potential is the one in the legacy file; whether a single radial potential can describe Taub-NUT at all is something to check against a reference.

Hopf fibres to OBJ

Choosing an imaginary unit u splits the 3-sphere into circles q ↦ e^{ut}q, a Hopf fibration. The units i, j and k give three fibrations, one for each of the hyperkähler triple's complex structures I, J, K, and every point lies on exactly one circle of each. Projected stereographically into 3D, the circles become interlocking rings. The export turns each ring into a closed quad tube, with one OBJ group per fibration and ring (I_ring_01, J_ring_01, K_ring_01, …), and scales the form to fit between −1 and 1 on every axis, centred at the origin.

Drag the preview to rotate it. Fibres that pass too close to the projection point would become enormous, so they are dropped and counted above.
