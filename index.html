Equivariance under u⊗x

Does f(u⊗x) equal u⊗f(x)? A right-multiplying layer keeps it, and so does a norm-based activation. A componentwise ReLU breaks it. Errors are ‖f(u⊗x) − u⊗f(x)‖ on random inputs.

Monge-Ampère on ℂ²

For a Kähler potential K(t) with t = r² = |z₁|² + |z₂|², the determinant is K′(K′ + tK″), and a Ricci-flat metric needs it constant. The legacy engine took a 1×1 Hessian in r instead, which is just φ″(r), and never compared it to anything.

Eguchi-Hanson uses its closed-form K′(t). Whether a single radial potential can describe Taub-NUT at all should be checked against a reference.
