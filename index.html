Equivariance under u⊗x

A layer that multiplies the input by a weight on the right, x⊗W, commutes with multiplying every input on the left by a unit quaternion u. A norm-based activation keeps that property. A componentwise ReLU breaks it. The numbers below are ‖f(u⊗x) − u⊗f(x)‖ on random inputs.

Monge-Ampère on ℂ²

For a Kähler potential that depends only on t = r² = |z₁|² + |z₂|², the complex Monge-Ampère determinant is K′(K′ + tK″). A Ricci-flat metric needs it to be constant. The legacy engine instead took a 1×1 Hessian in r, which is just φ″(r), and never compared it to anything.
